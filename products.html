Modular Industrial Polymer Racking

Engineered for extreme warehouse conditions, Plastiracks modular racking replaces corroding metal wire and angle iron. Constructed with high-density structural polymer, each unit delivers non-magnetic, chemical-resistant, and 100% rust-free storage.

  • Ventilated Grid Decks: Promotes uniform air circulation in walk-in cold rooms.
  • Flexible Tier Spacing: Adjustable increments without requiring specialty tools.
  • Chemical Invariance: Immune to saltwater, sanitizing foam, oils, and weak acids.
