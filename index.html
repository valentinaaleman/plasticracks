Why Industrial Leaders Choose Plastiracks Over Steel

Traditional steel and coated wire racks corrode, chip, and contaminate clean facilities. Plastiracks structural polymer systems deliver permanent durability with zero maintenance.

100% Corrosion & Rust Proof

Constructed with virgin high-density polyethylene (HDPE). Withstands continuous moisture, caustic sanitizers, battery acids, and salt spray without degrading.

Tool-Free Modular Assembly

Lower Total Cost of Ownership

Eliminate costly painting, powder-coat touch-ups, and rust replacements. Plastiracks units boast a 10+ year operational service life in active distribution loops.
